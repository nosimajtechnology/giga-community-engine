# $GIGA Culture and Rights Boundaries

## Keep three domains separate

### Original Gigachad source identity

The recognizable meme is associated with model Ernest Khalimov and images created by photographer Krista Sudmalis for SLEEK'N'TEARS. This identity predates the Solana token and must not be rewritten as a generic community-owned character.

### Verified $GIGA community culture

Official materials position $GIGA as a community-run Solana token built around the established Gigachad meme. Recurring official themes include:

- self-improvement and discipline
- masculinity and confidence/conviction
- training, pushups, accountability, and proof of work
- community participation and real-world culture
- `the big one`
- GIGA Fitness and fitness-lifestyle expression
- meme fluency and broad recognition beyond crypto-native audiences

Use those as cultural signals, not mandatory decorations. Prefer action that demonstrates discipline over repetitive slogans or generic gym-bro dialogue.

### Nosimaj creative transformation

Nosimaj may translate Gigachad into lost-game graphics, cinematic worlds, professions, fake commercials, absurd situations, and crossovers. These are creative interpretations—not retroactive original canon.

## Tone range

Support sincere, absurd, deadpan, cinematic, motivational, surreal, nostalgic, ordinary, and action-oriented work. Gigachad can train, repair a roof, wait for a bus, manage a motel, survive a strange world, star in a corporate ident, or appear in a quiet portrait.

Avoid:

- flattening every idea into bodybuilding or token-price jokes
- insecure, frantic, or boast-heavy copy when controlled action communicates confidence better
- unsupported claims that a remix, partnership, product, or community statement is official
- hateful or demeaning masculinity framing
- medical, hormone, supplement, or training claims presented as guaranteed fact

## Token and financial boundary

Creative uses may include `$GIGA`, community motifs, or fictional in-world branding when requested. Do not add them automatically.

Never produce token recommendations, price targets, guaranteed outcomes, return promises, urgency manipulation, or misleading financial endorsements. Label fictional ads as fictional when context could confuse viewers.

## Rights and attribution

- Do not claim that Nosimaj or a community remixer owns the underlying identity, source photos, name, marks, or third-party crossover IP.
- Do not grant an unrestricted license over third-party material.
- Do not bundle public photographs merely because they are easy to find.
- Use user-provided or clearly approved references within their assigned scope.
- Treat community creations as unofficial unless an authorized rights-holder designates otherwise.

Suggested credit when appropriate:
