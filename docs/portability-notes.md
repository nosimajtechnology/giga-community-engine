# Character Engine Portability Notes — GIGA v1

## Purpose

This private maintainer note records what transferred from the reusable community-character framework and what a recognizable human meme required. It is not the public installation guide.

## What generalized cleanly

- ordinary-language idea intake with automatic mode selection
- canonical reference → first frame → approval → storyboard → approval → animation prompt
- latest-approved-state authority and narrow repair
- provider-neutral creative brief before model adaptation
- progressive episode boards with explicit end-state carryover
- separate identity blocks for crossover characters
- deterministic Skill packaging and tag-gated release assets

## What GIGA required specifically

- strict separation of original source identity, generic Chad archetype, $GIGA culture, and Nosimaj transformation
- facial-geometry rules strong enough to prevent generic-bodybuilder substitution
- a game-era transformation ladder that preserves a human likeness at different polygon densities
- cultural range beyond repetitive gym jokes while retaining discipline, confidence, and self-improvement signals
- token/financial boundaries alongside creative community use

## What human likeness introduces

- small changes to jaw, brow, eyes, nose, hairline, beard, age, or facial width can break recognition
- photographic references carry subject, photographer, publicity, and derivative-use considerations not present in a wholly owned mascot
- stylization must simplify surfaces without discarding craniofacial relationships
- continuity failures are more noticeable because viewers are expert at reading human faces

## What meme-native identity introduces

- the audience recognizes both a person-like visual identity and an archetypal social meaning
- ironic usage, sincere aspiration, community slogans, and token culture overlap but are not interchangeable
- one-glance legibility matters; excessive lore or branding can weaken the meme
- official/community claims change over time and must be re-verified

## Style-transformation finding

The stable unit is not texture detail. It is the relationship among head silhouette, brow, eye depth, nose, jaw, hair mass, beard boundary, neck, shoulder width, waist, and controlled expression. Each console generation can reduce or increase surface fidelity while preserving that relational scaffold.

## Automation candidates

- reference-role manifest creation
- scene-state ledger extraction from approved boards
- prompt-length measurement and compression checks
- package validation, reproducible ZIPs, checksums, and releases
- storyboard duplicate-angle and screen-direction linting
- provider adapter selection from current capability metadata

## Judgment that remains human-led

- deciding whether a likeness is unmistakably Gigachad rather than merely Chad-like
- assigning legal/rights confidence to reference assets and public release choices
- interpreting community tone without flattening it into slogans
- deciding when transformation is faithful versus merely inspired
- selecting the strongest hook, shot, repair boundary, and final edit
- approving any public canonical reference set

## Commercial product implication

The method ports across mascot, fictional human/anime IP, and human meme identity when the engine has four independent layers: identity authority, cultural authority, transformation rules, and continuity state. The commercial Character Engine should template those layers, automate packaging and state tracking, and preserve an explicit human approval gate for likeness, rights, and taste.
